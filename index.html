What We Do

Industrial & Commercial Electrical Services

Industrial Electrical Installations

From factory floors to processing plants across the Ballarat region, we design and install three-phase systems, switchboards, and machinery wiring built to Australian Standards. Every install is documented, labelled, and handed over ready for audit.

Commercial Fit-Outs & Upgrades

We handle the full electrical scope for commercial premises — lighting, power, data, and emergency systems — coordinated around your trading hours to minimise disruption. Ideal for warehouses, workshops, and office-industrial hybrids.

Solar & Grid Connections

We manage commercial solar installations end-to-end, including the often-tricky grid connection process with your local network provider. We stay on the job until the meter's spinning the right way.

Switchboard Upgrades & Compliance

Older industrial switchboards are a common failure point and a serious safety risk. We upgrade and certify boards to current standards, including RCD protection, fault-current ratings, and clear circuit identification.

Preventative Maintenance & Testing

Scheduled testing, thermal imaging, and tag-and-test services that keep your operation compliant and your insurer happy. We work around production schedules so you're not losing hours to electrical downtime.

Need Something Else?

Industrial work doesn't always fit neat boxes. Give us a call and we'll talk through your project.
